## Description (4000)

Kastideo is a player: it brings your channels, series, movies and videos together in a simple, fast and customizable interface. **Kastideo does not provide, sell or host any content**: you add your own sources and you are responsible for their legality.

SUPPORTED SOURCES
• M3U / M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes subscriptions
• Jellyfin, Emby and Plex servers (synchronized resume)
• Folders, network shares and videos from your photo library
• YouTube videos through the official API

LIVE AND TV GUIDE
• Grid TV guide with zoom, category filters, favorites and themes
• Themes (sport, news, movies…), “tonight”, replay of past programmes when your provider allows it
• Programme alerts, recording and live timeshift
• Channel zapping in the player, swipe to change channel

MOVIES AND SERIES
• Cinema view with a featured banner, rows by category and detailed pages (poster, summary, cast, trailer with your free TMDB key)
• Resume where you left off, automatic next episode

PLAYER
• Playback speed, picture format, volume and brightness by gesture, AirPlay
• Background audio
• Technical stream information

CUSTOMIZATION
• Categories: order, hide, rename, hide by keyword
• Start page, light/dark theme, accent colour, poster size
• iCloud Drive sync between your devices (Mac, iPhone, iPad)
• No account, no ads, no trackers

IMPORTANT: Kastideo contains no channels or films. Use it only with content you have the right to access.
