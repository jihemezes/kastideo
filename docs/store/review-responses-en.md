## 5.2.3 — "audio/video streaming content / illegal file sharing"
Thank you for your review. Kastideo is a general-purpose media player. It does not include, offer, link to, or recommend any streaming content, channel list, subscription or provider, and it has no built-in catalog. Like other playlist players on the App Store, it plays only the sources that the user themself adds (an M3U playlist, a Jellyfin/Emby/Plex server on their own network, files on their device, or a subscription they already hold), and it shows a legal notice at first launch stating that the user is responsible for having the right to access the content. The app does not circumvent DRM, does not download or redistribute third-party content, and does not facilitate piracy. To test, use the "Try the demo" button, which adds a public playlist of royalty-free videos only. Please let us know if you need any additional information or a different test source.

## 2.1 — "we could not test / need demo content"
Thank you. No account or subscription is needed. On the Home screen (welcome screen on Apple TV), choose "Try the demo": it adds a public playlist of royalty-free test streams (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jihemezes/kastideo/main/docs/store/demo.m3u). If streams do not load on your network, the same playlist can be opened in any player to verify availability. We can provide a video of the full flow if useful.

## 4.2 / 4.3 — "minimum functionality / similar apps"
Kastideo is a native app with its own features, not a web wrapper or a template: TV guide grid with replay, live recording and timeshift, picture-in-picture-style multi-view, iCloud Drive sync, Jellyfin/Emby/Plex integration with shared watch progress, TMDB metadata, local library, AirPlay and Chromecast casting. It is developed and published by a single developer under the name Kastideo.

## 5.1.1 — "privacy / data collection"
The publisher collects no data. No account, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. The only network requests are those the user triggers toward their own sources, TMDB (with the user's own key) and YouTube (with the user's own key). Privacy policy: https://github.com/jihemezes/kastideo/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md

## 3.1.1 / 3.2.2 — "external payments / donations"
The App Store builds contain no donation link, no external purchase mechanism and no in-app purchase. The app is free.
